1. Opening & Self-Introduction (3 Menit)

"Assalamu’alaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh."

"Good morning, respected parents, teachers, and everyone present here. First of all, thank you for taking the time to be here and share this special moment with us."

(Selamat pagi, orang tua yang terhormat, guru-guru, dan hadirin sekalian. Pertama-tama, terima kasih telah meluangkan waktu untuk hadir dan berbagi momen spesial ini bersama kami).

"Today is not just an ordinary presentation. It is an opportunity for us to look back on our three-year journey at this school."

"Especially for the respected parents of [Nama Murid], we hope this presentation will give you a better understanding of [his/her] journey, [his/her] growth, and the person [Nama Murid] is becoming."

(Terkhusus kepada orang tua [Nama Murid], kami harap presentasi ini memberikan pemahaman yang lebih baik tentang perjalanannya, perkembangannya, dan menjadi sosok seperti apa ia saat ini).

"Because education is not only about grades or academic achievements. It is also about building character, discovering our potential, and preparing ourselves for the future."

"Please allow me to introduce myself. My name is Muhammad Taqwa Ridha. I am proud to be your moderator and supervisor today."

"Before we start, let's open this session by reciting Basmalah together.
Bismillahirrohmanirrohim."

"Now, let's welcome [Nama Murid] to start the presentation. [Nama Murid], the time is yours."

2. Sesi Presentasi (15 Menit)

Fokus mengamati dan menilai berdasarkan lembar rubrik serta bersiap mengisi link form supervisor.

Peringatan 5 Menit Terakhir

"Just a gentle reminder, [Nama Murid], you have 5 minutes left."

(Hanya mengingatkan, waktu kamu tersisa 5 menit lagi).
⚠️ Ucapkan ini jika perlu, atau gunakan isyarat yang sudah disepakati.
3. Q&A and Parent Appreciation (10 Menit)
(Setelah murid selesai presentasi)

"Alright, thank you [Nama Murid]. That was a very good presentation! Let’s give a big round of applause."

"Now, it is time for the Questions and Answers session. For [Nama Murid], please remain standing at the front."

(Sekarang saatnya sesi Tanya Jawab. Untuk [Nama Murid], silakan tetap berdiri di depan).
OPSI A (Waktu Normal)

"To the parents, we would love to hear from you. You can ask questions, give comments, or share your messages for [Nama Murid]. The time is yours."

OPSI B (Jika Presentasi > 15 Menit)

"To the parents, we would love to hear from you. Due to the limited time, we will take only one question or comment for this session. The time is yours."

💬 Biarkan interaksi tanya jawab antara anak dan orang tua berlangsung.
4. Documentation (Sesi Foto)
(Setelah sesi tanya jawab selesai)

"Thank you so much to the parents of [Nama Murid] for your kind words and support."

"Before we finish, let’s take a picture together for documentation. For the parents, please kindly come to the front."

(Untuk ayah dan ibu, dengan hormat silakan maju ke depan).
(Sambil mengarahkan posisi berdiri)

"[Nama Murid], you can stand in the middle."

(Saat memegang kamera/HP bersiap memfoto)

"Alright, look at the camera. Ready? I will take the picture in 3... 2... 1... smile! 📸"

"Let's take one more picture. 3... 2... 1... cheese! 📸"

5. Closing (2 Menit)
(Setelah berfoto, arahkan mereka duduk kembali)

"Thank you. For the parents, please have a seat."

(Tunggu orang tua duduk kembali, lalu tutup sesinya)

"Alright, we are done with this session. Thank you again to the parents of [Nama Murid] for coming today. We wish [Nama Murid] all the best in the future."

"Before we end this session, I apologize for any mistakes I might have made as your moderator today."

(Sebelum kita akhiri sesi ini, saya memohon maaf atas segala kekurangan saya sebagai moderator hari ini).

"Now, let's close this session by reciting Hamdalah together.
Alhamdulillahirabbil'alamin."

"Our session has ended, [Nama Murid] and parents may leave the room. Wassalamu’alaikum Warahmatullahi Wabarakatuh. Thank you, have a nice day!"

(Sesi kita sudah berakhir, [Nama Murid] dan Bapak/Ibu dipersilakan meninggalkan ruangan. Terima kasih, semoga hari Anda menyenangkan!).
